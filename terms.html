Purpose

The KITC Digital Management System provides a public project entry point and a protected administrative workspace for authorized KITC operations.

Authorized use

Access to protected areas is limited by the authentication and authorization controls configured by KITC. Users must not attempt to bypass those controls or access records outside their assigned role.

Records and responsibility

The application can manage institutional records such as members, meetings, tasks, events, attendance and documents. Operators are responsible for entering accurate information and following KITC's internal policies.

Security

The interface is not itself a security boundary. Production deployments should enforce authorization at the protected data layer and keep sensitive credentials out of client-side code.

Availability

The public site and application may be changed, maintained or temporarily unavailable. No specific availability level is promised by this public demonstration.
